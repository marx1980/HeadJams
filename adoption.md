# Adoption and reach

Goal: be the easiest way for a non-player to turn a hummed idea into a song they can share. Order of work is in `plan.md` (milestone E).

## Features
- **Song link.** Open a link and hear the song; one button opens it in the app to remix. The song data lives in the link or a static file; no server and no account.
- **Social export.** A short video (vertical and square) of the hummed take turning into the arrangement, with waveform or notes on screen.
- **Offline.** Network-off test in `npm test` or CI; "make available offline" for instruments not yet played; install prompt on supported browsers.
- **Challenges.** A fixed prompt (hum a melody, tap a beat) with a tag people can post under; built from song links.
- **Lesson kit.** Short printable and on-screen lessons for teachers (hum to song, chords by ear, build a chorus); works without accounts.
- **Localisation.** Croatian first, then other languages; text stays labels and values.
- **Accessibility.** Full keyboard use, screen-reader names, large tap targets, reduced motion.
- **Launch material.** One 60-second demo video, a README with a GIF, a Show HN and Product Hunt post, a page for music teachers and libraries.

## Measures (no tracking in the app)
Stars and forks, shared links opened (counted by the static host's own logs only if you enable them), lesson-kit downloads, press and teacher mentions.
