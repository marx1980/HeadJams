## NOW: Hum-to-music, on real instruments

Product goal: `hum.md`. Someone who can't play hums, beatboxes, taps or draws an idea, hears it on real instruments, chooses by ear and builds a song; the original is always kept. Sound engine: `expansion.md` (sampled instruments, guitar). Engine decisions: nylon guitar first, about 150 MB of on-demand downloads, CC-BY credits in Settings › About, bundled samples redistributable only (the repo is public), personal recording optional.

Humming and hand editing are equal, linked ways to make the same notes: hummed material lands on the grid as editable notes and steps, grid edits always win over later corrections, and the grid/sample workflow stays complete on its own.

Rule: every step must keep the capture → recognise → play path working (see `CLAUDE.md`).

## DECISIONS
- Web/PWA first; Capacitor for native shells.
- One Web Audio clock with look-ahead scheduling. Live playback and export must use the same performance logic (from E0).
- Old sound IDs are permanent. Synth presets remain available as Classic sounds.
- Bundled third-party audio must be redistributable (CC0, CC-BY, or similar) and ship with provenance and licence files. Everything else is local-only.
- Hum-to-music is the product goal; the sampler serves it. Captured originals and raw detections are never overwritten; generated material is always a separate, marked track.
- On-screen text is limited to labels and values (`skill.md`).
- PRs are merged through the REST API (`CLAUDE.md`).
- Strategy (2026-10-30): free, open, offline, no ads, no accounts, widest reach. Monetisation is later and light (own-recorded packs, school licences, tips, grants). `money.md` phases M2–M6 (Free/Full, Release, Label, publishing) are deferred; see `strategy.md`.
- No Events migration: bars are a layer on patterns and arrangement clips.

## DEFERRED
