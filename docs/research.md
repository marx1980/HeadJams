# Research Report: Mobile Music-Making Apps

> Based on general knowledge of the apps below, not live research. Verify before treating as final.

## Apps surveyed
GarageBand, FL Studio Mobile, BandLab, Soundtrap, Groovepad, Beat Maker Pro / Drum Pad Machine, Koala Sampler, Loopy Pro / Loopy HD, Caustic / Medly, Ableton Note / Launchpad. Also noted: Bandmate, Voloco, Splice.

## Common features
1. Pad or grid for triggering sounds.
2. Step sequencer, usually 16 steps per row, one row per instrument/channel.
3. Loop and clip launching, with quantized start and loop length in bars.
4. Per-channel mixer: volume, pan, mute, solo.
5. Tempo (BPM), metronome, swing.
6. Built-in sound library with genre packs, plus sample import.
7. Recording/sampling from the microphone, with trimming.
8. Simple effects: reverb, delay, filter.
9. Timeline/arrangement view.
10. Export and share (WAV/MP3), project save, cloud sync.
11. Piano roll or keyboard, with chord and scale assist.
12. Onboarding tutorials, preset beats and templates.

## UX patterns that work
- Opens straight to a playable surface; first sound within seconds.
- Big touch targets; colour-coded channels and loops.
- Progressive disclosure: simple view by default, advanced editor one tap deeper.
- One clock for everything, with a visible playhead.
- Interactive first-run tutorials with guided "tap here" overlays.
- Undo/redo and auto-save.

## Common pitfalls
- Too many panels on a small screen.
- Audio latency.
- Unclear loop-length controls.
- Overwhelming sound browsers.
